Production Disciplines & Machinery

Industrial Manufacturing Capabilities.

Engineered execution across six core print and fabrication disciplines in the UAE. We combine multi-unit commercial presses, CNC optical laser cutting, and instant-curing flatbed UV technology to deliver uncompromised quality.

Discipline 01 / Commercial Press

Offset Printing

High-volume commercial printing delivering precise micro-dot registration, uniform ink coverage, and true-to-life Pantone and CMYK color fidelity.

Our offset presses handle high-run marketing collateral, corporate annual reports, luxury editorial publications, and packaging inserts with minimal unit variance. We offer specialized finishing options including spot UV varnishing, blind debossing, foil stamping, and matte/gloss thermal lamination.

Resolution / Accuracy 2400 DPI Plate Imaging
Volume Range 500 to 500,000+ Units
Enquire About Offset Printing
Discipline 02 / Polymers & Structure

Acrylic Fabrication

Sub-millimeter CNC laser cutting, flame polishing, diamond edge beveling, thermoforming, and multi-component assembly.

We convert optical-grade cast and extruded acrylic (Plexiglas, Optix, Perspex) into structural display fixtures, architectural elements, illuminated signage, retail showcases, and industrial protective housings. Our edge finishes achieve crystal optical clarity through proprietary multi-pass diamond and flame treatments.

Cutting Tolerance ±0.05 mm Precision
Sheet Thickness 1 mm to 50 mm Cast
Enquire About Acrylic Fabrication
Discipline 03 / Direct-to-Substrate

UV Flatbed Printing

Instant ultraviolet-cured direct printing on rigid sheet metals, composite aluminum, tempered glass, wood, and acrylics.

Flatbed UV printing delivers exceptional color vibrancy, high opacity white-ink underlays, and textured tactile varnish layers. Because inks are polymerized instantaneously with UV light, the output is immediately cured, scratch-resistant, weatherproof, and resistant to UV fading.

Max Bed Capacity 2500 mm × 1300 mm
Media Compatibility Rigid & Flexible Media
Enquire About UV Printing
Discipline 04 / Packaging & Web

Flexo Printing

High-speed rotary flexographic output tailored for custom packaging boxes, corrugated kraft boards, and label substrates.

Flexographic printing provides economical high-speed runs with rapid drying water-based and solvent inks. Ideal for commercial shipping cartons, luxury product boxes, and industrial packaging where robust ink transfer and material flexibility are paramount.

Web Speed High Rotary Throughput
Substrates Kraft, Corrugated & Film
Enquire About Flexo Printing
Discipline 05 / Tactile & Industrial

Screen Printing

Heavy-deposit industrial screen printing producing unmatched pigment opacity, tactile texture, and environmental durability.

When visual elements must endure harsh chemical exposure, UV sunlight, or mechanical abrasion, screen printing delivers unmatched ink film thickness. Used widely for equipment control plates, metal instrument panels, industrial labels, architectural signage, and specialty textiles.

Ink Thickness 10-50 µm Heavy Deposit
Durability Rating Chemical & UV Resistant
Enquire About Screen Printing
Discipline 06 / On-Demand & Large Format

Digital Printing

High-resolution on-demand output tailored for short-to-medium runs, variable graphics, fine-art reproductions, and large-format architectural fabrics.

Eliminating plate setup times, our digital printing department offers rapid turnaround with micro-droplet piezo printheads. We produce vivid wall murals, trade show graphics, architectural banners, bespoke packaging prototypes, and on-demand marketing collateral with photorealistic precision.

Turnaround Same-Day / 24h Express
Max Width Up to 3.2 m Seamless
